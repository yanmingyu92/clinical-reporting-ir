# Narrative Summary of Demographics (Table 14.1.1)

## Table 14.1.1: Summary of Demographics (Safety Population)

The demographic characteristics of the safety population are summarized below based on data from 254 subjects across three treatment groups: Placebo (N=86), Xanomeline Low Dose (N=84), and Xanomeline High Dose (N=84).

### Age

The overall mean age of subjects in the safety population was 75.1 years (SD = 8.25), with a median of 76.0 years and a range of 51 to 89 years. Mean age was generally comparable across treatment groups: the Placebo group had a mean age of 75.2 years (SD = 8.59; median 76.0; range 52–89), the Xanomeline Low Dose group had a mean age of 75.7 years (SD = 8.29; median 77.5; range 51–88), and the Xanomeline High Dose group had a mean age of 74.4 years (SD = 7.89; median 76.0; range 56–88). The age distributions were well balanced across the three treatment arms.

### Sex

Overall, the majority of subjects were female, comprising 143 of 254 subjects (56.3%), while 111 subjects (43.7%) were male. The proportion of females was higher in the Placebo group (53 [61.6%]) and the Xanomeline Low Dose group (50 [59.5%]) compared with the Xanomeline High Dose group (40 [47.6%]). Conversely, the Xanomeline High Dose group had a somewhat higher proportion of males (44 [52.4%]) relative to the Placebo (33 [38.4%]) and Xanomeline Low Dose (34 [40.5%]) groups.

### Race

The study population was predominantly White, with 220 of 254 subjects (86.6%) identifying as White across all treatment groups. The distribution was consistent across arms: 74 (86.0%) in the Placebo group, 72 (85.7%) in the Xanomeline Low Dose group, and 74 (88.1%) in the Xanomeline High Dose group. Black or African American subjects comprised 23 subjects overall (9.1%), with 8 (9.3%) in the Placebo group, 6 (7.1%) in the Xanomeline Low Dose group, and 9 (10.7%) in the Xanomeline High Dose group. The remaining subjects belonged to other racial categories (data truncated in source).

### Overall Assessment

In summary, the demographic characteristics of the safety population were generally well balanced across the three treatment groups. The subjects were elderly (overall mean age approximately 75 years), predominantly female (56.3%), and predominantly White (86.6%). A modest numerical imbalance in sex distribution was observed, with a higher proportion of males in the Xanomeline High Dose group compared with the other two groups; however, no notable imbalances in age or race were apparent across treatment arms.
